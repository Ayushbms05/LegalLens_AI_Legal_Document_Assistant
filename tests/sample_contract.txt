RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. PARTIES
This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on January 1, 2026, by and between:
Landlord: Apex Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and
Tenant: Alex Johnson ("Tenant").

2. PREMISES
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the premises located at:
104 Oakwood Avenue, Apartment 3B, Springfield, IL 62701 ("Premises").

3. LEASE TERM
The term of this Agreement shall commence on February 1, 2026, and end on January 31, 2027 ("Lease Term").
Upon expiration, this Agreement shall automatically renew on a month-to-month basis unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to expiration.

4. RENT & PAYMENT
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $1,850.00 USD, due on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month.
If rent is not received by the fifth (5th) calendar day of the month, a late fee of $75.00 USD shall be assessed.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $1,850.00 USD as a security deposit.
The security deposit shall be held in an escrow account and returned to Tenant within thirty (30) days following the surrender of the Premises, less any deductions for damages beyond ordinary wear and tear.

6. USE AND OCCUPANCY
The Premises shall be used solely as a private residential dwelling. Tenant shall not sublease, assign, or license any part of the Premises without prior written consent from Landlord.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition. Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and major appliance maintenance, provided such damage is not caused by Tenant negligence.

8. PET POLICY
No animals, birds, or pets of any kind shall be kept on the Premises without Landlord's prior written permission and payment of an additional non-refundable pet fee of $300.00 USD.

9. TERMINATION AND DEFAULT
In the event of default or breach of any terms herein, Landlord may issue a written notice requiring cure within five (5) business days. Failure to cure entitles Landlord to terminate this Agreement and initiate eviction proceedings.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be construed and governed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois.
